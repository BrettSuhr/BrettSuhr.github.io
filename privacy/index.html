Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

A Game of Twos is made by Brett Suhr ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and how to delete it. The short version: the game only keeps what it needs to save your progress, run the friends and daily leaderboards, and give you what you buy. I don't sell your data.

Playing without a profile

Your progress, settings and in-progress puzzles are saved on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to me.

Your profile (optional)

When you make a profile, the game creates an anonymous account (no email or password) and stores, on my server:

  • the display name you choose, your friend code and your avatar
  • your progress (highest level), coins, chests and unlocked items
  • your daily puzzle results: time, streak, and the taps you made (used only to check the result is fair)
  • your friends list, and players you block or report

Other players can see your name, avatar, level and daily times on leaderboards; friends see you on the friends board.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you back up your progress with Sign in with Apple, I receive an identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email address (which may be an Apple private relay address). It is used only to restore your profile on a new phone.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. I never see your payment details. To add coins or Remove Ads to your account, my server receives the purchase's transaction ID and product from Apple and keeps a record so each purchase is credited once.

Ads

The game shows ads: videos you choose to watch for rewards, and occasional ads between levels (unless you buy Remove Ads). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device information such as an advertising identifier, your approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Ads are only personalized if you allow tracking when iOS asks. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Notifications

Daily streak reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No information is sent to send them.

Where it's stored

Profile data is stored with Supabase, my database provider. It is kept while your profile exists.

Deleting your data

In the game, open ☰ Settings and tap Delete account. Your profile and everything attached to it (progress, friends, daily results, items and purchase records) is deleted from the server right away. You can also email me and I'll delete it for you.

Children

The game is for a general audience. It doesn't ask for anyone's real name, email or age, and display names are filtered. If you believe a child has given personal information, email me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
