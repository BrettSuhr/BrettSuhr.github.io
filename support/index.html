Common questions

How do I play?

Tap a square to cycle it: empty, then one color, then the other. Fill the grid so that there are never three of the same color in a row, every row and column has as many black as white, squares joined by = match, and squares joined by × are opposite. Every puzzle has exactly one answer. You can replay the tutorial any time from ☰ Settings.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Open your profile (top left of the menu) and back up with Sign in with Apple. On the new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID and your progress comes back.

I bought coins but didn't get them

Coins are added once Apple confirms the purchase. If the game closed or lost connection, they're added automatically the next time you open it with a connection. Still missing? Email me with the date and your receipt from Apple (Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → Purchase History).

How do I restore Remove Ads?

Open ☰ Settings and tap Restore next to Purchases, while signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I add friends?

Open your profile from the menu. Share your friend code, or type in a friend's code.

Someone on the leaderboard is cheating

If they're your friend, tap them on your profile's friends board and choose Report. Players reported by several people are hidden from everyone else's boards. For anyone else, email me their name and the date.

How do I delete my account?

Open ☰ Settings and tap Delete account. Everything on the server is deleted right away. See the Privacy Policy.
