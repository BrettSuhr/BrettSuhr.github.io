Four rules.
One answer.

A two-color logic puzzle for iPhone. Fill the grid with black and white dots, and never guess.

  • No three of the same color in a row
  • Every row and column is half black, half white
  • = means the same color
  • × means opposite colors

1,000 levels from 6×6 to 12×12, a boss every 10 levels, chests full of unlockable colors, shapes and faces, your own dot avatar, and a new daily puzzle at midnight for everyone.
